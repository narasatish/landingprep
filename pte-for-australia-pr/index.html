PTE Score for Australia PR — Requirements & Points

PTE Academic is widely used for Australian skilled migration. The Department of Home Affairs converts PTE scores to the same English point tiers as IELTS.

The minimum you need

Competent English — PTE 50 in each skill (≈ IELTS 6.0) — is the usual minimum and adds 0 points.

The competitive (high-points) target

Proficient — PTE 65 in each (≈ IELTS 7.0) — adds 10 points; Superior — PTE 79 in each (≈ IELTS 8.0) — adds 20 points.

Why your score matters

PTE's computer scoring and fast (≈5 day) results make it a popular way to chase the 20-point Superior tier that lifts your SkillSelect rank.

Key tips

All four communicative skills must reach the threshold for the tier. PTE scores are valid 3 years for migration. Confirm current points rules with the Department of Home Affairs.

Frequently asked questions

What PTE score is 20 points for Australia PR?
PTE 79 in each of the four skills (Superior English, ≈ IELTS 8.0) adds 20 points; PTE 65 in each adds 10 points.
Is PTE easier than IELTS for Australia PR?
Many find PTE's computer-marked speaking less stressful and results faster, but difficulty is personal — both map to the same points tiers.
