IELTS Score for University of Tasmania

To study at University of Tasmania in Hobart, you typically need around IELTS Band 6 overall — about TOEFL 79 or PTE 58. Undergraduate courses may accept a little lower; competitive postgraduate programmes can ask for more.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for University of Tasmania?
University of Tasmania typically requires around IELTS Band 6 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~79 or PTE ~58). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.
Does University of Tasmania accept TOEFL or PTE too?
Yes — University of Tasmania also accepts TOEFL iBT (~79) and PTE Academic (~58) alongside IELTS.
