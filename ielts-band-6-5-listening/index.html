How to Get IELTS Band 6.5 in Listening

To score Band 6.5 in IELTS Listening you need about 27/40 correct out of 40. Here's the exact strategy — free.

▶ Take a free IELTS mock

Step-by-step plan

  1. Know the target. For Listening, Band 6.5 is about 27/40 correct out of 40 (boundaries shift slightly per test).
  2. Diagnose. Take a free IELTS Listening mock to see your current band and your specific weaknesses.
  3. Tactic 1. Read the questions and predict answers before the audio plays.
  4. Tactic 2. Obey the word limit (e.g. 'NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS').
  5. Tactic 3. If you miss one, let it go and keep listening — don't freeze.
  6. Tactic 4. Drill spelling, names and numbers — they are easy marks.
  7. Practise under timing. Do timed Listening sections until you hit about 27/40 correct out of 40 two or three times in a row.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for Band 6.5 in IELTS Listening?
You need about 27/40 correct out of 40. Every question is one mark with no negative marking, so never leave a blank.
Is Band 6.5 in Listening hard to get?
It's very achievable with focused, feedback-driven practice — most learners gain about 0.5 band in 4–6 weeks. The free mocks and AI checker show your progress.
