IELTS Score for University of Calgary

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for University of Calgary?
University of Calgary typically requires around IELTS Band 6.5 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~85 or PTE ~58). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.
Does University of Calgary accept TOEFL or PTE too?
Yes — University of Calgary also accepts TOEFL iBT (~85) and PTE Academic (~58) alongside IELTS.
