Frequently asked questions

Is the French course free?
Yes — the French A1 course, vocabulary with native-voice pronunciation, AI speaking practice and mock tests are completely free with no signup.
What French level do I need to study in France?
Most French-taught degrees want B2 (DELF/DALF or TCF). Campus France guides the level; English-taught programmes may need none. Start A1 here.
Which French exam is best for Canada?
For Canadian immigration, the TEF and TCF are accepted (Express Entry). For study, DELF/DALF and TCF are widely recognised.
