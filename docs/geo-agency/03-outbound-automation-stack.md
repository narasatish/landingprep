### Warmup Best Practices (2026)

1. **Do NOT rely on dashboard warmup scores alone.** Run Inbox Placement Tests (Mailmodo, Litmus, or mail tester services) weekly on sample sends.
2. **Domain warmup before sequences:** Warm for 7–10 days with light engagement before cold outreach. Start with 5 emails/day, ramp to 20–30.
3. **Multiple mailbox setup recommended:** Use 3–5 warmed sending addresses rotating per campaign to avoid reputation damage.
4. **SMTP relays for India:** If operating from India, consider SMTP relay providers (Postmark, Sendgrid, AWS SES) to bypass ISP throttling. Avoid shared IP pools.
5. **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** Non-negotiable. Misconfiguration causes 10–30% deliverability loss. Test with DMARC analyzer tools before sending.

## 7. LEGAL & COMPLIANCE RISK ASSESSMENT

### India: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

**Status:** In effect; enforcement Phase 1 began May 2023; full enforcement by May 2027  
**Relevant to cold email?** Possibly, depending on interpretation

#### DPDP Act Requirements

| **Requirement** | **Rule** | **Cold Email Impact** | **Compliance Action** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Consent** | Free, specific, informed, unconditional (no pre-checked boxes) | Must you get consent before cold email? UNCLEAR. Law doesn't explicitly cover business-to-business email. | Interpret conservatively: treat B2B email as non-personal data, but get consent for contact lists if they include personal IDs (phone, email linked to individual). |
| **Notice** | Standalone notice in plain language; 22 languages of India | If you collect data, provide transparent notice | Add unsubscribe footer + privacy policy to all emails. |
| **Data Minimization** | Collect only what you need | Do not cold email if you're only buying a list to spam. Have legitimate business reason. | Document why you're reaching out each prospect (e.g., "relevant to their industry/role"). |
| **Data Retention** | Delete personal data after purpose is served | Do not archive cold email lists indefinitely. | Delete email lists after 90–180 days if no conversion. |
| **Individual Rights** | Data Principals can request access/deletion | Must honor erasure requests within 30 days | Set up process to handle "unsubscribe" as deletion request under DPDP. |

#### Penalties
- Civil fine: up to ₹250 crore (~$30M USD) for serious violations
- Criminal liability: up to 5 years imprisonment (egregious cases)
- Enforcement: India's Data Protection Board (established in 2026)

#### DPDP + Cold Email Reality (2026 Interpretation)

**Honest assessment:**
- DPDP Act applies primarily to **personal data** (names, email, phone linked to individuals)
- B2B email to business addresses (company domain) may fall outside strict DPDP scope
- **However:** India's regulators are unclear; safer to assume compliance required
- No major cold email enforcement cases yet (as of July 2026)

**Safe approach:**

#### GDPR Penalties

- 2024 enforcement: €1.6B+ in fines; email marketing frequent cause

**Cold Email Under GDPR (2026 reality):**
- **For EU consumers:** DO NOT cold email. Full stop.
- **For EU businesses:** Borderline. Safest to avoid; if sending, warm up on LinkedIn first or get explicit consent.
- **For non-EU (including India):** GDPR does NOT apply

| **Physical address** | Must include company address in footer | $53,088/email | Add full address; use legitimate business address |
| **Unsubscribe link** | One-click unsubscribe; honor within 10 days | $53,088/email | Automate unsubscribe processing |
| **Honor opt-out within 10 days** | Must remove from list within 10 business days | $53,088/email | Set up automated suppression in email platform |
| **Identify commercial message** | Must say "Advertisement" if unclear | $53,088/email | Label cold email clearly |

#### CAN-SPAM Penalty Calculation
- **Per-email penalty:** $53,088/email (as of Jan 2025, FTC inflation adjustment)
- **Scale:** 1,000 emails with improper unsubscribe = $53.1M fine (unlikely but legal ceiling)
- **Realistic scenarios:** FTC has issued $30M+ settlements against companies with patterns of violations

**Cold Email Under CAN-SPAM (2026 reality):**

- **Real risk:** Operating your own mailbox without compliance infrastructure (no unsubscribe, fake address, deceptive subject)
- **Likelihood:** FTC has not targeted small cold email operators; focus on bulk email vendors

### Most Important Success Factors (2026)

1. **List quality > tool features.** Spend 50% of effort on targeting, 50% on execution. Bad list + great tools = 0 results.
2. **Deliverability first.** Cold email is an infrastructure sport. Warmup, SPF/DKIM, rotation matter more than copy.
3. **Test independent of dashboard.** Warmup scores, heat metrics are misleading. Run Inbox Placement Tests weekly.
4. **Compliance from day 1.** Add unsubscribe, privacy link, company address. Honor opt-outs within 10 days (CAN-SPAM) / 30 days (DPDP).
5. **Avoid LinkedIn automation.** HeyReach ban was March 2026. Cloud tools carry extreme risk. Use manual + VA if LinkedIn is critical.
6. **Don't oversell AI SDR tools.** They assist with research and copy; they don't replace sales discipline.
7. **Pivot quickly.** If reply rate <0.5% after 2 weeks, stop and retest messaging/list. Don't waste 3 months on broken campaigns.
