# GEO Competitors, Tools & Pricing Benchmarks (2026)

> The competitive landscape. **Key honest finding: the "India has no GEO agencies" idea is already
> outdated** — India GEO agencies exist (upGrowth, Nico Digital, PageTraffic, Stackmatix, DigiMark,
> GetCito). Our real whitespace is the **study-abroad / edtech niche**, not GEO in general.

---

## 1. GEO tracking tools / platforms (software we could use OR replace with our own engine)

Pricing verified ~July 2026:

| Tool | Entry price | Coverage / notes | Positioning |
|------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Otterly.ai** | **$29/mo** (Lite: 15 prompts, 4 engines) | Cheapest; simple citation monitoring | SMB / entry |
| **Peec AI** | **$95/mo** (50 prompts, 3 of 6 models, unlimited seats) | Mid-market team tracking | Agencies/teams |
| **Profound** | **$99/mo** (billed yearly, ChatGPT only, 1 seat) → scales to 10 engines, "Agents" action layer, SOC 2, SSO | **Raised $96M Series C at ~$1B valuation, Feb 2026; 700+ enterprise customers** | Enterprise leader |
| **Scrunch** | **$250/mo** (all 7 engines incl. Claude + Meta on every plan, action layer) | Widest engine coverage on every tier | Mid-market, full coverage |
| **Peec/Otterly alternatives** | varies | Ayzeo, Surmado, Loamly, Licheo, Rankscale, Trakkr, Knowatoa, AthenaHQ, Goodie AI, Daydream | Crowded, fast-moving |
| **Semrush / Ahrefs AI modules** | bundled ($$$-$$$$) | AI visibility added to existing SEO suites | Enterprise SEO teams |

**Implication for us:** the tracking-tool layer is crowded and commoditizing (starts at $29). We should
**NOT compete as a tool** — we build our own lightweight tracking engine in-house (our stack already
has a Gemini proxy) and sell the **service + strategy**, using tools only where cheaper than building.

## 2. GEO service agencies

**Global players:** Position Digital, Stratabeat, First Page Sage, Citable, Optimist, Rank Shift,
plus traditional SEO agencies bolting on GEO. USP themes: proprietary tracking, "Share of Answer"
metrics, case studies (claims of 13x-49x LLM referral/revenue growth).

**India players (already active):** upGrowth, Nico Digital, PageTraffic, Stackmatix (India arm),
DigiMark, GetCito, Sqroot, InfluencerHai (edtech-focused digital, expanding to GEO).

**How they get clients:**
- **Inbound (dominant):** ranking for "GEO agency" / "best GEO agencies", "best AEO tools" listicles,
  proprietary research/data studies, directory listings (Clutch/G2), LinkedIn thought leadership.
- **Outbound (secondary):** targeted cold email/LinkedIn to SaaS + D2C marketing leads.
- Content marketing + "eat your own dog food" (being cited in AI themselves) is the signature move.

## 3. Pricing benchmarks

**India GEO retainers (2026):**
| Tier | Monthly (₹) | What it buys |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Starter | ₹55K-₹80K (some quote ₹75K-₹1.25L) | 1 strategist, part-time content, audit-led optimization |
| Growth | ₹90K-₹1.5L | dedicated squad + citation tracking across 3-5 AI platforms |
| Scale | ₹1.6L-₹2.6L | + original research, proprietary data, PR outreach, schema at scale |
| Enterprise | ₹3L+ | full program |

Most serious engagements land **₹90K-₹1.6L/mo**. Contracts commonly **month-to-month after a 90-day
initial commitment**. **India pricing runs ~40-60% below comparable US agencies** for the same scope.

**Audits:** typically sold as a paid entry/tripwire (₹5K-₹15K in India; higher globally), often
credited toward the first retainer month.

## 4. Market gaps / whitespace (where WE win)

1. **Vertical specialization — study-abroad / edtech / coaching.** India GEO agencies are generalist.
   None own the study-abroad/edtech vertical, which we understand deeply from our own sites. This is
   the defensible angle.
2. **Productized, low-friction entry** — a free instant AI-visibility audit + a fixed-price starter
   package, vs. the enterprise-sales motion most agencies run.
3. **Proof by demonstration** — get our OWN sites cited in AI search as live evidence competitors
   can't show.
4. **India price advantage** — deliver at ₹55K-₹1.5L with AI-automated delivery keeping our costs low.

**Honest risk:** the category is maturing fast (Profound at $1B; India agencies already ranking).
First-mover advantage in *GEO generally* is largely gone — our edge must be **niche + automation +
proof**, not merely "we do GEO."
