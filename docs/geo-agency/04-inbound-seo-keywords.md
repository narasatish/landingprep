## 2. Content strategy (what ranks AND gets cited)

Formats that win (same as delivery playbook): comparative tables/listicles (32.5% of AI citations),
statistical roundups (+40%), how-to checklists, expert definitions, 2,000+ word guides (3x citations),
case studies with metrics. HTML tables cited 2.5x; being on 4+ platforms = 2.8x more likely cited in ChatGPT.

**Flagship pieces to publish (our own site):**
1. **"2026 GEO Agency Buyer's Guide"** — tables, pricing, ROI, platform differences. Targets "GEO agency", "best GEO agencies".
2. **"How to Get Cited by [ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews]"** — one post each.
3. **"State of AI Search 2026: EdTech Visibility Report"** — PROPRIETARY research (our differentiator + PR magnet).
4. **Case studies with quantified metrics** — "How [brand] got 26x more ChatGPT citations in 12 weeks".
5. **Reddit/community presence** — r/SEO, r/MarketingStrategy, edtech + study-abroad subreddits.

Sales-enablement stat to reuse: AI-referred traffic converts ~15.9% (ChatGPT) / 10.5% (Perplexity)
vs 1.76% Google organic.

## 4. Eat our own dog food (be the proof)
12-week sprint to get our own site cited in AI search:
- **Wk 1-2:** schema (`Organization`, `ProfessionalService`, FAQ), entity consistency, (optional) llms.txt.
- **Wk 2-4:** publish 2-3 flagship pieces (buyer's guide, EdTech research, platform how-to).
- **Wk 4-6:** seed 4+ platforms (LinkedIn, Reddit, G2/Capterra reviews, guest posts); pursue backlinks from "best GEO agencies" lists.
- **Wk 6-8:** track brand mentions in ChatGPT/Perplexity/AIO; optimize if not cited.
- **Wk 8-12:** publish case studies + free GEO audit tool (lead magnet → capture → nurture).
