UK Student Visa Interview Questions (Credibility Interview) 2026

Genuine, consistent answers — not memorised scripts — are what pass a visa interview. Use these as practice prompts, then answer in your own words.

▶ Plan your study abroad free
⚡ Quick answer: Many UK Student visa applicants are asked to attend a short “credibility interview” (by video or in person) where a UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) officer checks that you are a genuine student. They assess whether you understand your course and university, can fund your studies, and have made an informed choice — not whether you've memorised answers. Speak naturally in English about your real reasons and plans.

How the Student visa (credibility interview) assessment works

After getting your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor and meeting the financial and English requirements, UKVI may invite you to a credibility interview, often conducted over video call. It is assessed on whether you are a genuine student making a credible choice. There are no trick questions — the officer simply wants consistent, informed answers in understandable English.

Course & university

Why did you choose this course and university?
💡 Give specific, personal reasons — modules, reputation in your field, location, career relevance. Show you researched it rather than picked it at random.
What modules will you study and what's the course structure?
💡 Know the basics: length, key modules, assessment style. This is the most common place under-prepared applicants slip up.
What other universities did you consider?
💡 It's fine to have considered others — explain honestly why you chose this one.

Why the UK & your background

Why study in the UK rather than your home country or elsewhere?
💡 Connect it to your field and goals (e.g. the one-year master's, specific expertise, industry links). Avoid generic answers.
How does this course fit your previous studies and career plan?
💡 Show a logical progression from what you've done to what you'll do next.

Finances

How will you fund your tuition and living costs?
💡 Know your tuition, the UKVI maintenance funds requirement, and exactly where the money comes from (savings, loan, sponsor). Your answer must match your financial evidence.
Who is your sponsor and what is their occupation?
💡 State clearly and consistently with your documents.

After your studies

What do you plan to do after you graduate?
💡 You may mention the Graduate Route (post-study work), but give a genuine, thought-through plan rather than a vague one.

Why applications get refused

  • Not knowing basic details of your own course or university (the most common failure).
  • Answers that contradict your application, CAS or financial evidence.
  • English that's too weak to understand the questions or respond clearly.
  • Vague or generic reasons for choosing the UK, the course or the university.
  • Signs the choice isn't genuine — e.g. a course unrelated to your background with no explanation.

How to prepare

  1. Re-read your own application, CAS and personal statement before the interview.
  2. Be ready to describe your course modules, fees and funding from memory.
  3. Test your camera, microphone and internet beforehand if it's a video interview.
  4. Answer in your own words and in clear English — genuine beats rehearsed.
  5. Keep your documents (CAS, financial proof, qualifications) to hand during the call.

⚠️ Answer honestly — never script or fake it

Visa officers are trained to spot rehearsed or false answers, and inconsistency with your documents is the fastest way to be refused. Use these questions to practise and to make sure your real reasons, finances and plans are clear and consistent — never to invent a story. Always confirm current requirements on the official government website before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

Does every UK Student visa applicant get an interview?
No. UKVI selects many applicants for a short credibility interview, often by video, but not everyone is interviewed. Prepare as if you will be.
What is the UK credibility interview checking?
That you are a genuine student: that you understand and have genuinely chosen your course and university, can fund your studies, and meet the English requirement.
What happens if I fail the credibility interview?
Your visa can be refused on credibility grounds even if your paperwork is fine. That's why knowing your course, finances and reasons genuinely — not by script — matters.
