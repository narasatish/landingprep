🎯 What's a good PTE score?

  • Score 50–58: accepted by most Australian universities and some UK institutions
  • Score 65+: competitive for top universities; visa requirement for Australia/UK skilled migration
  • Score 79+: excellent; required for professional registration (nursing, engineering, accounting)

💡 PTE prep tips

  • Speaking: enunciate clearly and maintain steady pace—machine-scored, so clear pronunciation is critical; background noise is automatically detected and may lower score
  • Reading: practice re-ordering paragraphs using logical flow and transition words as clues—this section trips many test-takers
  • Listening: the writing-from-dictation task rewards careful listening—use headphones, listen actively, and replay if available
