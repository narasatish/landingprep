🎯 What's a good IELTS score?

  • Band 6.0–6.5: accepted by most universities; entry-level for UK/Australia visas
  • Band 7.0–7.5: competitive for top 50 universities; preferred by professional bodies (nursing, engineering)
  • Band 8.0+: advanced fluency; required for graduate research, leadership roles

💡 IELTS prep tips

  • Listening: familiarize yourself with all three accents (British, American, Australian) used in IELTS tests—train on podcasts and documentaries
  • Reading: strict time management—allocate 18–20 minutes per passage to avoid rushing; skim headings and topic sentences first
  • Speaking: prioritize fluency and coherence over perfect grammar; examiners reward natural speech patterns and relevant examples
