📋 TOEFL iBT exam pattern (2026)

Total time: approximately 2 hours — iBT format (shortened July 2023); no breaks between sections; integrated tasks test multiple skills per task · Scoring: 120 (composite: 0–30 per section)

SectionTimeQuestions / tasksWhat it tests
Reading35 minutes2 passages (10 questions each)Academic texts (620–680 words); comprehension, vocabulary, inference, main idea; multiple choice, select-all-that-apply, drag-and-drop
Listening36 minutes3–4 conversations + 2–3 lecturesAcademic conversations (2–3 min) and lectures (3–5 min); understanding main idea, details, inference, speaker attitude; multiple choice, select-all-that-apply
Speaking12 minutes4 tasksTask 1: independent (15 sec prep, 45 sec response); Tasks 2–4: integrated (read/listen, then speak 30–60 sec)
Writing29 minutes2 tasksTask 1: integrated (read 3 min + listen 2 min, write 20 min, 150–225 words); Task 2: writing for an academic discussion (30 min, 100+ words)

Format reflects the current TOEFL iBT as of 2026 — always confirm details on the official site before your test.

🎯 What's a good TOEFL score?

  • Score 80–90: accepted by most US universities; entry-level for graduate programs
  • Score 100–110: competitive for top 50 universities; preferred by strong master's programs
  • Score 110+: excellent for PhD, research positions, postdoctoral roles

💡 TOEFL prep tips

  • Reading: use skimming and scanning techniques—read topic sentences and the first question before diving into full text
  • Listening: take organized notes (abbreviations, key terms) during lectures—you can reference them during questions
  • Writing: in integrated tasks, synthesize information from reading and listening; in academic discussion, respond conversationally to peers while maintaining academic tone
