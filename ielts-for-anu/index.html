Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for Australian National University?
Australian National University typically requires around IELTS Band 6.5 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~80 or PTE ~64). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.
Does Australian National University accept TOEFL or PTE too?
Yes — Australian National University also accepts TOEFL iBT (~80) and PTE Academic (~64) alongside IELTS.
