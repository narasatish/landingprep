What IELTS score do I need for University of Groningen?
University of Groningen typically requires around IELTS Band 6.5 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~85 or PTE ~58). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.

Yes — University of Groningen also accepts TOEFL iBT (~85) and PTE Academic (~58) alongside IELTS.
