Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for Stanford University?
Stanford University typically requires around IELTS Band 7 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~100 or PTE ~68). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.
Does Stanford University accept TOEFL or PTE too?
Yes — Stanford University also accepts TOEFL iBT (~100) and PTE Academic (~68) alongside IELTS.
