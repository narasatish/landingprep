How IELTS One Skill Retake works

  • One skill only: retake Listening, Reading, Writing OR Speaking — just one, once, per full test.
  • Computer-delivered only: your original test must have been IELTS on computer, at a centre that offers OSR (selected centres/countries).
  • 60-day window: you must sit the retake within 60 days of your full test — this covers both booking and taking it.
  • New Test Report Form: you receive a fresh TRF with the improved skill plus your other three original scores.
  • Cheaper than a full retake: roughly a quarter of the full fee (about INR 12,650 in India at the time of writing — confirm the current amount with your centre).

The catch: check acceptance first

IELTS itself advises confirming that the organisation you're applying to accepts a One Skill Retake result before you book. Many universities do, but some — and several visa/immigration routes — still require a single full IELTS sitting. A quick email to the admissions or visa office saves you a wasted retake.

How to book

  1. Confirm your original test was computer-delivered and your centre offers One Skill Retake.
  2. Check the receiving university/authority accepts OSR.
  3. Use the calculator above to pick the single skill that reaches your target.
  4. Book through IDP or British Council within 60 days of your full test.

Frequently asked questions

What is IELTS One Skill Retake?
IELTS One Skill Retake (OSR) lets you retake just one section of the IELTS — Listening, Reading, Writing or Speaking — instead of sitting the whole test again. You receive a new Test Report Form showing the improved skill alongside your other three original scores.
Who is eligible for IELTS One Skill Retake?
You must have taken your full IELTS as a computer-delivered test at a centre that offers OSR, and you must sit the retake within 60 days of your original test. It is available on computer only, in selected centres and countries. You can retake only one skill, once, per full test.
How long do I have to book One Skill Retake?
You must take your One Skill Retake within 60 days of your full IELTS test date — this deadline applies to both booking and completing the retake.
Is IELTS One Skill Retake accepted everywhere?
No — not every university, employer or immigration authority accepts an OSR result. IELTS advises you to confirm directly with the organisation you are applying to before booking. Many visa/immigration routes still require a single full test sitting.
How much does One Skill Retake cost?
It is cheaper than a full test — roughly a quarter of the full IELTS fee (about INR 12,650 in India at the time of writing). Fees change and vary by centre, so confirm the current amount with IDP or British Council.
Which skill should I retake?
