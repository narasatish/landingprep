Frequently asked questions

Which are the best universities for MS Data Science in USA?

How much does MS Data Science in USA cost?
Tuition is typically $30,000–60,000/yr plus living costs of $12,000–20,000/yr. Scholarships and education loans can cover a large part.
What are the requirements for MS Data Science in USA?

Yes — USA offers OPT 12 months (36 for STEM). H-1B work visa → employer-sponsored Green Card (EB-2/EB-3). STEM OPT bridges the gap. PR timeline: Green Card: 1–10+ yrs (category & country dependent).
