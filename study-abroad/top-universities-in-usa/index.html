🛂 Student visa, post-study work & PR in USA

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈75% — F-1 student visa; approval varies by profile and consulate.
  • Visa types: [object Object]; [object Object]; [object Object]; [object Object]
  • Post-study work: OPT 12 months (36 for STEM)
  • Immigration pathway: H-1B work visa → employer-sponsored Green Card (EB-2/EB-3). STEM OPT bridges the gap.
  • Settlement / PR: Green Card backlog is long for Indians; EB-1/NIW faster for high achievers. (Green Card: 1–10+ yrs (category & country dependent))

PR pathway, step by step

  1. Enter on an F-1 visa, maintain status and intern via CPT during your degree.
  2. On graduation, apply for OPT — STEM graduates get a 36-month work window.
  3. Have an employer file an H-1B in the lottery (or use cap-exempt / O-1 routes).
  4. Employer sponsors your Green Card (PERM → I-140 → I-485); EB-1 or NIW is faster if you qualify.
  5. After 5 years as a Green Card holder, apply for US citizenship.

🎯 Tips to get admission & visa in USA

  • Apply early. Submitting 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
  • Clear the English score first. Most USA programmes need IELTS 6.5–7.0 — take a free mock to find your weakest section before booking.
  • Show clear, stable funds. Visa officers want genuine proof of funds covering tuition ($30,000–60,000/yr) plus living costs.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact programme and university — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Pick the right intake. Main intakes: Fall (Aug–Sep) & Spring (Jan).
