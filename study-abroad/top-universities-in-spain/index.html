Admission essentials for Spain

  • English test: IELTS 6.0 (or equivalent TOEFL/PTE).
  • Tuition: €1,000–3,500/yr (public); €7,000–16,000/yr (private) for international students.
  • Intakes: September, February.
  • Documents: SOP, 2–3 LORs, transcripts, CV, and a strong Statement of Purpose — build yours free with our SOP tool.

Visa, immigration & settlement in Spain

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈76% — Spanish student visa approvals are solid; financial proof and enrollment confirmation are key requirements.
  • Post-study work: Residence permit available post-graduation; 12-month job-search extension possible; work permit with employment
  • Immigration pathway: Spain does not offer explicit post-study work visa but grants residence permits to graduates seeking employment. Securing a job allows transition to work residency. PR eligibility after 5 years continuous legal residence with employment.
  • Settlement / PR: Strong food culture, vibrant social life, affordable cost of living, and warm community integration. (Permanent residency after 5 years continuous legal residence)
  • Cost of living: €700–1,100/month outside Madrid/Barcelona; €1,100–1,500/month in major cities

Recent changes (2024–26)

  • 2025: Spain introduced a 12-month post-study residence permit extension for Master's graduates, improving job-search runway.
  • 2026: Bilateral agreements signed with India offering expedited work permits for engineering and IT Master's graduates at top Spanish universities.
