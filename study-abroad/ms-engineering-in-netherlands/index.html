How much does MS Engineering in Netherlands cost?

What are the requirements for MS Engineering in Netherlands?
Most programs require IELTS 6.5 (or TOEFL/PTE equivalent), a strong GPA, an SOP and LORs; some ask for the GRE/GMAT. Confirm on each university's official page.
Can I work in Netherlands after MS Engineering?
Yes — Netherlands offers Orientation Year (zoekjaar): 1 year. Highly Skilled Migrant permit (salary-based) → PR after 5 years. PR timeline: PR: ~5 yrs.
