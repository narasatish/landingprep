Admission essentials for Czech Republic

  • English test: IELTS 5.5 (or equivalent TOEFL/PTE).
  • Tuition: €2,000–4,500/yr (most); €7,000–16,000/yr (Medicine) for international students.
  • Intakes: September, February (limited).
  • Documents: SOP, 2–3 LORs, transcripts, CV, and a strong Statement of Purpose — build yours free with our SOP tool.

Visa, immigration & settlement in Czech Republic

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈81% — Czech student visa approvals are reliable; immigration procedures are transparent and efficiently processed.
  • Post-study work: Residence permit available post-graduation; 12-month job-search window; work visa upon employment
  • Immigration pathway: Czech Republic grants residence permits to employed graduates. After 5 years continuous legal residence with stable employment, PR is accessible. Non-EU citizens follow streamlined pathways.
  • Settlement / PR: UNESCO-protected historic center, vibrant youth culture, affordable rent and food, and efficient public transport. (Permanent residency after 5 years continuous legal residence)
  • Cost of living: €500–800/month (nationwide); Prague slightly elevated

Recent changes (2024–26)

  • 2025: Czech Republic formalized 12-month post-study residence permit for Master's graduates, automatically granted upon graduation without additional visa fees.
  • 2026: New bilateral scholarship scheme with India: 300+ full-tuition grants for Medicine and Engineering Master's; priority for NEET/JEE qualifiers.
