Admission essentials for Australia

  • English test: IELTS 6.5 (or equivalent TOEFL/PTE).
  • Tuition: AUD 44,000–50,000/yr for international students.
  • Intakes: February, July.
  • Documents: SOP, 2–3 LORs, transcripts, CV, and a strong Statement of Purpose — build yours free with our SOP tool.

Visa, immigration & settlement in Australia

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈80% — Subclass 500; Genuine Student test applies.
  • Post-study work: Temporary Graduate (485): 2–4 years
  • Immigration pathway: Skilled Independent (189), State Nominated (190), Regional (491) — points-based.
  • Settlement / PR: Permanent residence via skilled points (age, English, work, qualification). (PR: ~2–4 yrs)
  • Cost of living: AUD 21,000/yr

Recent changes (2024–26)

  • 2024: Genuine Student (GS) test replaced the GTE requirement.
  • 2025: Savings requirement raised to AUD 29,710; higher English bands.
  • 2025: International-student commencement caps under discussion.
