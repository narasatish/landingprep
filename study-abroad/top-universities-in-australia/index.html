🛂 Student visa, post-study work & PR in Australia

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈80% — Subclass 500; Genuine Student test applies.
  • Visa types:
    • Subclass 500 (Student) — Study; work up to 48 hours per fortnight.
    • Subclass 485 (Graduate) — 2–4 years of post-study work rights.
    • Subclass 189/190/491 — Points-based skilled — independent, state-nominated or regional.
    • Permanent Residence — Granted via the skilled points test (age, English, work, study).
  • Post-study work: Temporary Graduate (485): 2–4 years
  • Immigration pathway: Skilled Independent (189), State Nominated (190), Regional (491) — points-based.
  • Settlement / PR: Permanent residence via skilled points (age, English, work, qualification). (PR: ~2–4 yrs)

PR pathway, step by step

  1. Pass the Genuine Student test and study on a subclass 500 visa.
  2. Apply for the 485 Graduate visa; build skilled work and raise your English score.
  3. Lodge an Expression of Interest in SkillSelect and complete a skills assessment.
  4. Receive an invitation under 189/190/491 → grant of permanent residence.
  5. Apply for citizenship after 4 years' residence (at least 1 as a PR).

🎯 Tips to get admission & visa in Australia

  • Apply early. Submitting 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
  • Clear the English score first. Most Australia programmes need IELTS 6.5 — take a free mock to find your weakest section before booking.
  • Show clear, stable funds. Visa officers want genuine proof of funds covering tuition (AUD 35,000–50,000/yr) plus living costs.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact programme and university — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Pick the right intake. Main intakes: February & July.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for universities in Australia?
Most universities in Australia require IELTS 6.5 overall for postgraduate admission, with some competitive programmes asking for 7.0+. Use the free LandingPrep College Predictor to match your exact score to universities.

When are the intakes in Australia?

Australia has an indicative student-visa success rate of about 80%. Subclass 500; Genuine Student test applies. Strong proof of funds and a genuine study plan matter most.
