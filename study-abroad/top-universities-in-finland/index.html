Admission essentials for Finland

  • English test: IELTS 6.0 (or equivalent TOEFL/PTE).
  • Tuition: €0–8,500/yr (EU); €12,000–20,000/yr (non-EU); scholarships waive for international students.
  • Intakes: August, January.
  • Documents: SOP, 2–3 LORs, transcripts, CV, and a strong Statement of Purpose — build yours free with our SOP tool.

Visa, immigration & settlement in Finland

  • Student-visa success rate: ≈83% — Finnish student visa has high approval rates; authorities are transparent and efficient in document review.
  • Post-study work: 12-month residence permit for job-search post-graduation; extendable to 24 months if employment secured
  • Immigration pathway: Finland recognizes skilled talent; after securing a job matching your Master's qualification, work permit and residence are granted. After 4 years continuous legal residence, apply for permanent residency with minimal additional scrutiny.
  • Settlement / PR: Exceptional education system spillover, high wages, strong social safety net, and innovative culture make long-term residency attractive. (Permanent residency after 4 years continuous legal residence)
  • Cost of living: €900–1,300/month outside Helsinki; €1,100–1,500/month in Helsinki

Recent changes (2024–26)

  • 2025: Finland increased post-study work visa to 12 months for all Master's graduates, with explicit option to extend another 12 months upon job offer.
  • 2026: Fast-track permanent residency pathway introduced: graduates in critical sectors (AI, green tech, biotech) eligible for PR after 2.5 years continuous employment.
