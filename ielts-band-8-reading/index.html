How to Get IELTS Band 8 in Reading

To score Band 8 in IELTS Reading you need about 35/40 correct out of 40. Here's the exact strategy — free.

▶ Take a free IELTS mock

Step-by-step plan

  1. Know the target. For Reading, Band 8 is about 35/40 correct out of 40 (boundaries shift slightly per test).
  2. Diagnose. Take a free IELTS Reading mock to see your current band and your specific weaknesses.
  3. Tactic 1. Skim for structure first, then scan for keywords and synonyms.
  4. Tactic 2. Spend about 20 minutes per passage; leave the hardest for last.
  5. Tactic 3. Master True/False/Not Given vs Yes/No/Not Given logic.
  6. Tactic 4. Locate, decide, move on — don't read every word.
  7. Practise under timing. Do timed Reading sections until you hit about 35/40 correct out of 40 two or three times in a row.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for Band 8 in IELTS Reading?
You need about 35/40 correct out of 40. Every question is one mark with no negative marking, so never leave a blank.
Is Band 8 in Reading hard to get?
It's very achievable with focused, feedback-driven practice — most learners gain about 0.5 band in 4–6 weeks. The free mocks and AI checker show your progress.
