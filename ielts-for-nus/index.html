IELTS Score for National University of Singapore (NUS)

To study at National University of Singapore (NUS) in Singapore, you typically need around IELTS Band 6.5 overall — about TOEFL 92 or PTE 65. Undergraduate courses may accept a little lower; competitive postgraduate programmes can ask for more.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score do I need for National University of Singapore (NUS)?
National University of Singapore (NUS) typically requires around IELTS Band 6.5 overall (equivalent to TOEFL iBT ~92 or PTE ~65). Undergraduate courses are often 0.5 lower; some postgraduate programmes ask higher — confirm on your course page.
Does National University of Singapore (NUS) accept TOEFL or PTE too?
Yes — National University of Singapore (NUS) also accepts TOEFL iBT (~92) and PTE Academic (~65) alongside IELTS.
