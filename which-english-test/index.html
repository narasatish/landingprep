Frequently asked questions

Which English test is the easiest?
It depends on your strengths and destination. PTE and Duolingo are fully computer-scored and fast; IELTS has a real-person speaking test; TOEFL is the US favourite. Our free quiz recommends one based on your answers.
IELTS or TOEFL — which should I take?
Choose TOEFL for the USA or if you prefer an all-computer test; choose IELTS for the UK, Australia, Canada, Ireland and the widest global acceptance.
Is the Duolingo English Test accepted?
Yes — thousands of universities accept the Duolingo English Test. It's the cheapest and fastest option, but always confirm your specific university accepts it.
Which test is best for Canada PR?
For Canadian Express Entry, IRCC accepts IELTS General, CELPIP-General and PTE Core (not TOEFL). CELPIP is purpose-built for Canada.
