IELTS Score for Doctors

To register with AHPRA / the Medical Board of Australia, international medical graduates registering to work in Australia must prove English. The requirement is: IELTS Academic 7.0 in each of the four skills.

▶ Take a free IELTS mock test

The requirement

  • IELTS: IELTS Academic 7.0 in each of the four skills.
  • Alternative: OET at grade B in each section is accepted.
  • Use IELTS Academic (not General Training) for professional registration.

Always confirm the current rule on the official regulator's website — requirements change.
