IELTS Score for Toronto Metropolitan University

To study at Toronto Metropolitan University in Toronto, ON, you typically need around IELTS Band 6.5 overall — about TOEFL 87 or PTE 59. Undergraduate courses may accept a little lower; competitive postgraduate programmes can ask for more.

Yes — Toronto Metropolitan University also accepts TOEFL iBT (~87) and PTE Academic (~59) alongside IELTS.
