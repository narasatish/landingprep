What Band 6 requires in Reading

IELTS Academic Reading is 3 long passages with 40 questions in 60 minutes — and there is NO extra transfer time. General Training Reading uses shorter, everyday texts but the same band boundaries.

For Band 6 specifically: you need about 23/40 correct out of 40.

Step-by-step plan to Band 6 in Reading

  1. Know the target. For Reading, Band 6 is about 23/40 correct out of 40 (boundaries shift slightly per test).
  2. Diagnose. Take a free IELTS Reading mock to see your current band and your specific weaknesses.
  3. Tactic 1. Skim for structure first, then scan for keywords and synonyms.
  4. Tactic 2. Spend about 20 minutes per passage; leave the hardest for last.
  5. Tactic 3. Master True/False/Not Given vs Yes/No/Not Given logic.
  6. Tactic 4. Locate, decide, move on — don't read every word.
  7. Practise under timing. Do timed Reading sections until you hit about 23/40 correct out of 40 two or three times in a row.

Common mistakes that keep you below Band 6

  • Reading every word instead of skimming then scanning — the #1 reason people run out of time.
  • Confusing True/False/Not Given with Yes/No/Not Given (statement of fact vs writer's opinion).
  • Spending too long on the hardest passage and rushing the easy marks at the end.
  • Copying an answer straight from the text but with a spelling slip.

How to reach Band 6 faster

Practise finding answers by synonym (the text rarely repeats the question's exact words), time each passage to roughly 20 minutes, and drill the logic of True/False/Not Given.

💡
'Not Given' means the passage neither confirms nor contradicts the statement — students lose marks by guessing 'False' when the text simply doesn't mention it.

The arithmetic of Band 6 in Reading

In Reading, Band 6 is 23 correct out of 40. Band 6.5 is 27 — 4 more questions. That is the whole distance, and it is worth seeing as a number rather than as a band: 4 questions is typically one careless transfer error, one misread instruction, and one question abandoned under time pressure. Half a band is usually not a knowledge problem.

Because there is no negative marking, a blank is strictly worse than a guess — every unanswered question is a guaranteed zero where a guess is not. Boundaries also shift slightly between test versions, so treat 23/40 as the working target and aim a question or two above it rather than exactly at it.

How section bands become your overall band. Your overall IELTS band is the average of all four section bands, rounded to the nearest whole or half band — and the rounding runs upward at the quarter. An average of 6.75 is reported as 7.0; an average of 6.5 stays 6.5; an average of 6.6 is reported as 6.5, not 7.0. The practical consequence is that Reading is worth exactly one quarter of your result, and lifting your weakest section by half a band moves your average by 0.125 — often not enough on its own. If you need a whole band overall, two sections have to move, and it is usually cheaper to take your two weakest up than to push an already-strong section higher.

Where most institutions actually set the bar. Many universities and nearly all migration and registration routes publish a per-section minimum alongside the overall figure. Where they do, your lowest section is what counts and a strong average will not rescue it. Check whether the requirement you are working toward is "overall 6" or "6 in each" before you build a study plan around it — they call for genuinely different preparation.
