📝 Step-by-step admission process

  1. Shortlist programs & note GRE/GMAT policy
  2. Take IELTS/TOEFL + GRE/GMAT if required
  3. Prepare SOP, 2–3 LORs, CV & transcripts

🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

  • Apply early. With around 76% acceptance, the main risk is late or incomplete applications — submit early for the best shot at scholarships and housing.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program and 1–2 professors/labs at George Mason University and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Hit the English score first. George Mason University needs IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 80 / PTE 59. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
  • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition (~$26k/yr for international graduate students) plus living costs ($12,000–20,000/yr); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
  • Apply in the right intake. George Mason University's main intakes are Fall (Aug) & Spring (Jan) & Summer (June) — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
