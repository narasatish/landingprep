  1. Shortlist programs & check GRE policy

🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

  • Apply early. With around 80% acceptance, the main risk is late or incomplete applications — submit early for the best shot at scholarships and housing.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program at Langara College and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Hit the English score first. Langara College needs IELTS 6 / TOEFL 80 / PTE 50. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
  • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition ($16.9k/yr international) plus living costs (CAD 12,000–18,000/yr (CAD 20,635 for visa)); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
  • Apply in the right intake. Langara College's main intakes are Fall (Sep) & Winter (Jan) & Spring (May) — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
