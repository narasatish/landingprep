📝 Step-by-step admission process

  1. Find English-taught master's (Studielink)
  2. Meet IELTS 6.5 + GRE if recommended
  3. Apply by Apr 1 (non-EU)
  4. Receive offer → pay deposit
  5. Arrange proof of funds
  6. Apply for MVV residence permit

🛂 Student visa & post-study work · Netherlands

  • Student visa: Residence permit (MVV)
  • Work while studying: 16 hrs/week during term
  • Post-study work: Orientation Year (zoekjaar): 1 year

🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

  • Beat the bar. With about 35% acceptance, aim above the minimums — target IELTS 7+ and a strong GPA, and lead with research, projects or publications.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program and 1–2 professors/labs at University of Amsterdam and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Hit the English score first. University of Amsterdam needs IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 85 / PTE 58. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
  • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition (€15–18k/yr) plus living costs (€10,800–13,200/yr); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
  • Apply in the right intake. University of Amsterdam's main intakes are Sep — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
