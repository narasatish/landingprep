    • Student visa: National (D) visa + residence permit
    • Work while studying: ≈15 hrs/week during term (from 6 months in)
    • Post-study work: 6-month permit to seek work after graduation

    🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

    • Beat the bar. With about 30% acceptance, aim above the minimums — target IELTS 7.5+ and a strong GPA, and lead with research, projects or publications.
    • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program and 1–2 professors/labs at EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
    • Hit the English score first. EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) needs IELTS 7 / TOEFL 100 / PTE 68. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
    • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition (≈ CHF 1,460/yr (very low)) plus living costs (CHF 22,000–27,000/yr (high cost of living)); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
    • Apply in the right intake. EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne)'s main intakes are Fall (Sep) & Spring (Feb) — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
