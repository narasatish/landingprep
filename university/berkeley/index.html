📝 Step-by-step admission process

  1. Shortlist programs & note GRE/GMAT policy
  2. Take IELTS/TOEFL + GRE/GMAT if required
  3. Prepare SOP, 2–3 LORs, CV & transcripts
  4. Apply online (Fall deadlines Dec–Jan)
  5. Pay application fee; submit
  6. Receive decision → I-20 → F-1 visa

🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

  • Beat the bar. With about 11% acceptance, aim above the minimums — target IELTS 7.5+ and a strong GPA, and lead with research, projects or publications.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program and 1–2 professors/labs at University of California, Berkeley and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Hit the English score first. University of California, Berkeley needs IELTS 7 / TOEFL 90 / PTE 68. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
  • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition ($30–38k/yr) plus living costs ($12,000–20,000/yr); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
  • Apply in the right intake. University of California, Berkeley's main intakes are Fall (Sep) — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
