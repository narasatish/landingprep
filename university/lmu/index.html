📝 Step-by-step admission process

  1. Find English-taught master's (DAAD/uni-assist)
  2. Meet IELTS 6.5 + GRE if required; get APS
  3. Apply via uni-assist or university portal
  4. Receive admission letter
  5. Open blocked account (≈€11,904)
  6. Apply for German student visa
