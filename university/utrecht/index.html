🎯 Tips & tricks to get admission

  • Beat the bar. With about 40% acceptance, aim above the minimums — target IELTS 7+ and a strong GPA, and lead with research, projects or publications.
  • Write a tailored SOP. Name the exact program and 1–2 professors/labs at Utrecht University and connect your background to them — generic essays are the #1 reason strong profiles get rejected.
  • Hit the English score first. Utrecht University needs IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 85 / PTE 58. Take a free full-length mock to find your weakest section before you book the real test.
  • Prepare finances early. Budget for tuition (€17–19k/yr) plus living costs (€10,800–13,200/yr); visa officers want clear, stable proof of funds.
  • Apply in the right intake. Utrecht University's main intakes are Sep — applying 4–6 months ahead keeps scholarships and on-campus housing open.
