About LandingPrep

From mock test to campus abroad — free exam prep and a complete study-abroad toolkit for international students.

Our mission

Premium exam preparation and study-abroad guidance should not cost a fortune. LandingPrep exists to give every international student — regardless of budget or country — free, high-quality tools to prepare for the IELTS, TOEFL iBT, PTE Academic, CELPIP, Duolingo English Test, GRE and GMAT Focus, and to navigate the entire journey of studying abroad: choosing a country and university, winning scholarships, writing a strong SOP, comparing costs and loans, and understanding visas and immigration.

What we offer — all free

  • 5,400+ practice questions in full-length mock tests across 7 exams, with real timings, speaking & writing practice and model answers.
  • College Predictor across 99 top universities with Safe/Target/Reach matches, fees and requirements.
  • Scholarship finder, SOP/LOR/resume builders, university & country comparison, loan & ROI calculators.
  • Visa-interview practice and step-by-step immigration & PR roadmaps for 9 destinations.

Editorial standards & how we verify content

  • Fact-checking: Exam formats, scores, fees, deadlines and visa rules are verified against official sources — test makers (IELTS, ETS, Pearson), university websites and government immigration portals — with sources linked inline.
  • Currency: Guides are reviewed regularly and re-dated whenever the underlying exam format, fees or visa rules change.
  • Independence: We are not affiliated with any test provider or university, and we never publish pay-to-rank placements.
  • No invention: We never fabricate statistics, reviews, author credentials or success stories. Where a figure is indicative (e.g. visa-success rates), we say so explicitly.
  • Who writes and reviews this: Guides are researched, written and cross-checked by the LandingPrep editorial team, with every factual claim traced to a primary official source. We publish under the team name rather than attach invented "expert" bylines or credentials we cannot stand behind — what we ask you to trust is the linked source next to each fact, not a name.
  • Corrections: Found something out of date or wrong? Email support@landingprep.com; we verify, fix and re-date the page.
