IELTS Score for Ireland: Study & Visa Requirements

For Ireland, the typical IELTS requirement is 6.0–6.5 overall (often 6.5 for postgraduate). Accepted by all Irish universities; the Stamp 1G graduate route lets you stay and work after study.
