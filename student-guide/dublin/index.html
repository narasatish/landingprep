🇮🇪 Student Guide to Dublin

Dublin is a vibrant, English-speaking destination with excellent universities and a legendary social scene. It offers a blend of historical charm and modern culture, though prices have risen in recent years.

▶ Pre-departure checklist & visa timeline

Cost & getting around

  • Monthly budget: EUR 1,200-1,700 per month (accommodation EUR 700-1,000, food EUR 250-350, transport EUR 80, entertainment EUR 170-300)
  • Transport: Dublin Bus and Luas (light rail) are the main systems. A student leap card costs around EUR 20-25/week and works on all public transport, including taxis and ferries.
  • Where students live: Ballymun and Drumcondra (affordable, residential, good bus links), Rathmines (artsy, cafes, nightlife, good transport), Temple Bar area (vibrant but pricey, full of tourists), Stoneybatter (trendy, emerging food scene, good pubs)

Tips for students in Dublin

  • Get a Leap Card (student version) for discounted transport across all modes
  • Visit Temple Bar (busy and touristy) but explore quieter pubs in other neighborhoods
  • Shop at Aldi, Lidl, and Dunnes Stores for budget groceries
  • Explore free attractions like Trinity College campus, St. Stephen's Green park, and the Guinness Storehouse discounts with student ID
  • Attend university events and pub quizzes—Dublin's social scene revolves around pubs
