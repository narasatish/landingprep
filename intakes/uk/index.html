Intake seasons in UK

Most UK degrees start in September or October. A growing number of universities also offer a January intake, useful if you miss the autumn deadline.

Application timeline — work backwards from your intake

  1. 12–18 months before: shortlist universities and take a free IELTS / PTE / TOEFL mock to find your gap, then book the real test.
  2. 8–12 months before: prepare your SOP and recommendation letters, finalise your test scores, and submit applications — apply early for scholarships.
  3. 3–6 months before: accept your offer, arrange proof of funds, and apply for your student visa (prepare for the visa interview).
  4. 1–3 months before: book travel and accommodation, and complete pre-departure steps.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main intake in UK?
The main intake in UK is September/October, with a smaller January intake at many universities. Most UK degrees start in September or October. A growing number of universities also offer a January intake, useful if you miss the autumn deadline.
When should I apply for a UK university?
Start 12–18 months before your intake: take your English/admission tests early, then apply roughly 6–12 months ahead. Top universities and scholarships close first, so earlier is always safer.
Are these UK deadlines exact?
They're indicative typical deadlines and vary by university and programme — always confirm the exact date on each university's official admissions page before applying.
