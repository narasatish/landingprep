Education Loan for Studying Abroad: How to Choose & Compare Lenders (2026)

A clear guide to education loans for studying abroad — collateral vs non-collateral, interest rates, and how to compare lenders and EMI.

▶ Free College Predictor & study-abroad tools

Quick answer

Education loans for abroad study come in two forms: secured (with collateral, lower rates, higher limits up to ₹1.5 crore) and unsecured (no collateral, faster, smaller limits, higher rates). Compare interest rate, processing fee, moratorium, repayment tenure and margin — not just the headline rate — and always estimate your EMI first.

Secured vs unsecured

Secured loans from public banks (SBI, BoB, PNB) offer the lowest rates (~10.5–11.5%) and biggest limits but need property/FD collateral and take longer. Unsecured loans from NBFCs (HDFC Credila, Avanse, Auxilo) and international lenders (Prodigy, MPOWER) are faster and need no collateral, at higher rates.

What to compare

Interest rate (fixed vs floating), processing fee, moratorium (repayment holiday during study + grace), total repayment tenure, margin money you must fund yourself, and prepayment terms. A lower rate with a long moratorium can beat a slightly cheaper rate without one.

Tax & documents

In India, education-loan interest is deductible under Section 80E. Keep your admission letter, fee structure, co-applicant income proof and collateral documents ready to speed up sanction.

International lenders

Prodigy Finance and MPOWER offer no-collateral, no-co-signer loans in USD for students at top global universities — useful when domestic collateral isn't an option, though rates are higher.
