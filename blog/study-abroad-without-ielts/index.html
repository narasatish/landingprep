Quick answer

Yes, many universities accept alternatives to IELTS — TOEFL, PTE, the Duolingo English Test, or a waiver if you studied previously in English (a medium-of-instruction or MOI letter). Some also accept a video interview. Always confirm the exact policy with each university before applying.

Accepted alternatives

TOEFL iBT and PTE Academic are accepted almost as widely as IELTS. The Duolingo English Test is cheaper and at-home, accepted by 5,500+ institutions. Confirm minimum scores per program.

English-medium waiver (MOI)

If your previous degree was taught entirely in English, many universities waive the English test with a Medium of Instruction letter from your institution. Policies vary widely — get it in writing from the admissions office.

Country notes

Several universities in the USA, UK, Canada, Germany, Ireland and Australia offer waivers or accept alternatives, but student visas (UKVI, etc.) may still require a Secure English Language Test. Don't assume a university waiver covers the visa.

Don't skip prep
