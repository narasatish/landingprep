Canadian study permit now requires CAD 22,895 in a GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate) for SDS applicants in 2025. Learn how to open with ICICI, SBI Canada, CIBC, or other providers—and how funds release monthly.

What Is a GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate)?

A GIC is a low-risk financial product that proves you have sufficient funds to study in Canada. Canadian immigration authorities use it as proof of finances for international students applying through the SDS (Student Direct Stream) pathway.

Unlike a blocked account (Germany) or savings deposit (USA), a GIC is a genuine investment: you deposit money with a Canadian bank or fintech, and it grows at a fixed interest rate (typically 4–5% per year) over a set term (usually 1 year). After maturity, you receive your principal + interest.

For study permit purposes, the GIC remains in place while you study; monthly disbursements are released to cover living expenses. When you graduate or leave Canada, the remaining balance is returned to you with accrued interest.

2026 GIC Amount for Canadian Study Permit

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) specifies the required amount based on your program length and living costs.

💰
For 2025–26: CAD 22,895 for Year 1 + tuition. This covers 1 year of living expenses (food, rent, transport, books). Confirm the exact 2026 amount on the official IRCC website.
  • The amount is per year of study. A 2-year master's program requires ~CAD 45,790 (CAD 22,895 × 2).
  • Some provinces (Ontario, BC, Alberta) have slightly different requirements—check your provincial government website.
  • SDS applicants (from eligible countries including India) benefit from a streamlined process: GIC + valid passport + proof of admission = faster approval (~2 weeks).
  • Non-SDS applicants face stricter scrutiny and may need additional proof of funds (parental bank statements, property deeds, etc.).

Step-by-Step: Opening a GIC for Study Permit

Here's a detailed walkthrough using ICICI Bank Canada (most Indian-friendly).

  1. Visit ICICI Bank Canada's website (icicibankcanada.ca) and click 'Open a GIC' or 'Student GIC.'
  2. Fill out the online application form with your full name, date of birth, passport number, email, and contact address in India.
  3. Verify your email and confirm your identity. ICICI may request a copy of your passport (upload via secure portal).
  4. Select 'GIC for Study Permit' as the account type, and enter the amount (CAD 22,895 for 1 year, or CAD 45,790 for 2 years, etc.).
  5. Choose the GIC term (usually 1 year recommended, as you'll need access when you arrive in Canada).
  6. Provide your proof of admission to a Canadian college or university (acceptance letter, LOA—Letter of Acceptance).
  7. Fund the GIC via wire transfer from your Indian bank. ICICI Canada will provide wire instructions. Typical wire transfer fee from India: ₹500–₹2,000.
  8. ICICI verifies your transfer (allow 2–5 business days for the wire to clear).
  9. Once funds are received, your GIC is activated. You'll receive a GIC certificate via email, showing the investment amount, interest rate, maturity date, and account number.
  10. Use this GIC certificate + account statement as proof of funds in your study permit application (submit to IRCC online or with your paper file).
  11. After arrival in Canada and enrollment confirmation, contact ICICI to authorize monthly withdrawals (typically CAD 1,718/month for year 1).
  12. Each month, funds are transferred to your spending account (you'll set up a regular Canadian chequings account with ICICI or another bank).

How GIC Funds Are Released During Your Studies

The GIC is NOT a lump-sum disbursement. Here's the release mechanism:

  • Before arrival: GIC is locked and held by the bank. You cannot touch it.
  • After arrival & enrollment: You contact the bank and authorize the release schedule. Standard release = monthly (CAD 1,908/month for a CAD 22,895 GIC over 12 months).
  • Proof of study: To release funds, you must provide proof of enrollment each month (or semester). Typically a letter from your college/university confirming you're a full-time student.
  • Transfer to your account: Released funds go to your primary Canadian bank account (chequings), from which you draw for rent, groceries, transport, etc.
  • Interest accrual: While the GIC is active, it earns interest (4–5% per year). This interest is added to your account when you withdraw funds or the GIC matures.
  • Early withdrawal penalty: If you withdraw before the GIC matures (or leave Canada early), you may forfeit some interest. Check your GIC terms.
  • Final payout: When the GIC matures (usually 1 year after deposit), any remaining balance is paid to you along with accrued interest.

Funding Sources: How to Get CAD 22,895

Where does the CAD 22,895 come from? Several options:

  • Parents' savings: Most common. Parents transfer CAD 22,895 from their Indian bank to ICICI Canada. Provide a family relation affidavit.
  • Education loan: Indian lenders like HDFC Credila, Avanse, Auxilo offer education loans for Canada, often covering the full GIC amount. See our education loan guide.
  • NRI parent in Canada: If your parent is already working in Canada, they can deposit into the GIC directly (fastest, no wire transfer needed).
  • Combination: Partial loan + partial parental funds. E.g., education loan covers CAD 10,000, parents contribute CAD 10,635.
  • Scholarship reduction: If you have a partial scholarship (e.g., CAD 5,000/year), you still need the GIC to cover the remaining CAD 15,635.

Timeline & Costs: What to Budget

Here's a realistic timeline and all costs involved:

  • Account opening: 1–2 weeks (ICICI) to 3–4 weeks (CIBC/RBC). Start 8–10 weeks before your study permit application deadline.
  • Wire transfer: ₹500–₹2,000 fee from your Indian bank. Wise is often cheaper (₹200–₹400) but takes an extra 2–3 days.
  • Exchange rate: INR-to-CAD conversion has a 1–3% spread. Budget 2.5% extra (~₹40,000 for a CAD 22,895 deposit).
  • GIC interest: 4–5% per year earned on your deposit. Over 1 year, you earn CAD 826–1,032 in interest (free money!).
  • Account maintenance: Most Canadian banks charge $0–$5 CAD/month for student accounts. ICICI is often free.
  • Study permit processing: FREE for SDS applicants. Paper-based non-SDS is ~CAD 150.
  • Total all-in cost: CAD 22,895 + ₹40,000 (~CAD 700) for fees + transfer costs = ~CAD 23,595 budgeted. You get interest back, so net cost is lower.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Many Indian students derail their study permits here. Don't be one of them:

  • Opening a GIC after applying for study permit: IRCC needs proof of funds BEFORE approval. Open the GIC first, then apply.
  • Linking a savings account instead of a GIC: A regular savings account won't work. IRCC requires a formal GIC certificate from a regulated bank.
  • Wrong amount: Depositing CAD 15,000 when CAD 22,895 is required will trigger a rejection or request for additional funds.
  • Forgetting to authorize monthly releases: Many students arrive in Canada and discover their GIC is still locked because they never called the bank. Do this within 1 week of enrollment.
  • Missing the SDS deadline: SDS requires application within a specific time window. Missing it forces you into the regular stream (4–6 weeks processing instead of 2 weeks).
  • Not providing proof of enrollment: When requesting monthly releases, you must provide a letter from your college confirming full-time enrollment. Get this from your registrar's office ASAP.
  • Losing the GIC certificate: Keep a digital copy in your phone, email, and cloud storage. You'll need it to prove funds and authorize releases.

Next Steps: From GIC to Study Permit to Arrival

Here's the full sequence to follow:

  • Week 1–2: Open GIC with ICICI Bank Canada (or your chosen provider).
  • Week 2–3: Receive GIC certificate and account statement via email. Download and save these.
  • Week 3–4: Apply for study permit online via IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada). Upload GIC certificate + acceptance letter + passport + valid ID.
  • Week 4–6: Receive approval notification (SDS is ~2 weeks; regular stream is ~4–6 weeks). Letter will have your study permit number.
  • Week 6–8: Book your flight to Canada. Open a bank account online with ICICI, RBC, or CIBC (many allow pre-arrival accounts).
  • Week 8: Arrive in Canada. Visit your college's international student office to confirm enrollment and get your official enrollment letter.
  • Week 9: Contact your GIC provider and submit your enrollment letter to authorize monthly withdrawals.
  • Week 10+: Start receiving monthly disbursements. Read our Canada student visa guide for the full documentation checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the Canadian GIC amount for study permit in 2026?
The required GIC amount for 2025–26 is **CAD 22,895 for 1 year** of study. This includes living expenses (~CAD 16,000) + tuition (~CAD 4,635). Confirm the exact 2026 figure on the official IRCC website before applying.
Which GIC provider is best for Indian students?
**ICICI Bank Canada** is the most Indian-friendly due to its ICICI India partnership, Hindi-language support, and fast 1–2 week processing. **SBI Canada** (Astro Canada) is also good but slower (2–3 weeks).
Can my parents' money fund the GIC, or does it have to be my own savings?
Yes, your parents' funds are completely fine. The GIC is in your name, but the source is typically parental savings. Provide a family relation affidavit if IRCC asks.
How long does the GIC remain locked while I study?
After you arrive in Canada and enroll, the GIC is 'unlocked' for monthly releases (~CAD 1,718/month). It is NOT fully locked for the entire duration. You access it as soon as you provide proof of enrollment.
What happens to the GIC money after I graduate?
When your study program ends, the GIC is closed and any remaining balance (plus accrued interest) is returned to you. If you completed a 1-year master's and used all funds, the GIC is returned empty. If you used less, you get the balance back.
Do I earn interest on the GIC?
Yes! GICs typically earn 4–5% per year. On CAD 22,895, you'll earn CAD 916–1,145 in interest over 1 year. This interest is added to your account when you withdraw or the GIC matures.
What is SDS (Student Direct Stream), and do I need it?
SDS is a fast-track study permit program for students from eligible countries (including India). With a GIC + valid passport + acceptance letter, your application is approved in ~2 weeks instead of 4–6 weeks. It's highly recommended.
Can I open a GIC without an acceptance letter from a Canadian college?
No. Most GIC providers require proof of admission (an acceptance letter or LOA from a Canadian institution). Get admitted first, then open the GIC.
What if I need more than CAD 22,895 because my program is 2 years or I have dependents?
For a 2-year program, you need CAD 41,270 (double). If you have a spouse or dependent, add more for each family member (IRCC publishes a family-size table; a single applicant is CAD 22,895). Adjust your GIC deposit accordingly and submit updated documentation to IRCC.
