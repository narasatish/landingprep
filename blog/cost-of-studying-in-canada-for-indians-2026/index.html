Study Permit & GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate) Requirement

To obtain a Canadian study permit, Indian students must demonstrate financial support through a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) held in a Canadian bank, or proof of funds in a Canadian bank account.

As of 2024, the GIC requirement has been removed for most countries, but International Mobility Program (IMP) and provincial requirements may still apply. Confirm current requirements on Government of Canada's official website.

⚠️
GIC and proof-of-funds requirements change frequently. Verify current Study Permit requirements on canada.ca before submitting applications. As of June 2026, many countries may have relaxed GIC requirements—confirm with your province and institution.

Work Hours & Part-Time Earnings in Canada

International students can work up to 20 hours per week during the academic term and full-time (40+ hours) during scheduled breaks (summer, winter). This significantly helps offset living costs.

💡
Unlike the USA, Canada allows off-campus work without additional permits. Many students work part-time in restaurants, retail, or tutoring to earn CAD 12,000–18,000 annually.
  • Minimum wage (varies by province): CAD 14–16.55/hour (BC, Ontario, Alberta 2026)
  • Part-time on-campus jobs: CAD 16–20/hour (library, dining, administration)
  • Off-campus retail/food service: CAD 15–18/hour
  • Monthly earnings (20 hours/week): CAD 1,120–1,440 (₹0.67–0.86 lakhs)
  • Annual earnings (part-time + breaks): CAD 12,000–18,000 (₹7.2–10.8 lakhs), covering 20–30% of costs

Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) & Pathway to Permanent Residency

Canada's Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) is one of the most valuable benefits for Indian students. Graduates can work legally in Canada for 1–3 years after graduation, and this work experience counts toward Canadian permanent residency (PR) applications through the Express Entry system.

Provinces with Lowest Cost of Living

For budget-conscious Indian students, these provinces offer the best value:

  • Quebec: Lowest tuition (CAD 16,000–22,000/year) and lowest living costs (Montreal CAD 1,230–1,770/month)
  • Atlantic Canada: Halifax, St. John's, and other Atlantic cities offer tuition at CAD 12,000–17,000/year and living costs under CAD 1,600/month
  • Alberta: Calgary and Edmonton combine reasonable tuition (CAD 15,000–20,000/year) with affordable housing (CAD 1,450–2,000/month)
  • Avoid: Vancouver and Toronto due to high housing costs (CAD 1,200–2,000/month for rent alone)

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to study in Canada per year?
Annual costs range from **CAD 25,000–55,000** (approximately **₹15–33 lakhs**), depending on the province and program. This includes tuition (CAD 15,000–28,000), accommodation (CAD 9,000–14,400), and living expenses (CAD 4,200–6,600).
What is the GIC requirement for a Canadian study permit?
As of 2024, **GIC requirements have been removed for most countries**, including India. However, you must still demonstrate proof of funds for tuition and living expenses. Confirm current requirements on **canada.ca** and your institution's website.
Can Indian students work part-time in Canada?
Yes, **study permit holders can work up to 20 hours/week during the academic term** and full-time during scheduled breaks. Off-campus work is permitted (unlike the USA). Typical earnings: CAD 12,000–18,000 annually.
Which Canadian province is cheapest for international students?
**Quebec and Atlantic Canada** are the most affordable. Quebec tuition is CAD 16,000–22,000/year with Montreal living costs at CAD 1,230–1,770/month. Atlantic cities (Halifax, St. John's) are even cheaper.
How long is the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) in Canada?
PGWP duration is equal to the length of your eligible study program: **up to 3 years for a 2-year master's degree**. See [Canada PGWP 2026 Guide](/blog/canada-pgwp-2026-guide/) for full details.
What is the cost difference between studying in Canada vs USA?
**Canada is 20–30% cheaper**: Canada averages CAD 30,000–50,000/year (₹18–30 lakhs) vs. USA at USD 30,000–60,000/year (₹25–48 lakhs). Additionally, Canada offers PGWP work hours and easier PR pathways.
Do I need an education loan to study in Canada?
Many Indian students use education loans to cover 70–90% of costs. Indian banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) offer loans at 7–10% interest with 7–15 year repayment periods. Combined with on-campus work, loans make Canada very affordable.
What is the tuition for business and engineering programs in Canada?
**Business and engineering programs cost 20–30% more** than liberal arts: typically **CAD 22,000–32,000/year** (₹13–19 lakhs) in major universities, compared to CAD 15,000–20,000 for other fields.
