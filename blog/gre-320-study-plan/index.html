GRE 320+ Study Plan: How to Score High on Quant & Verbal (Free)

A structured plan to reach GRE 320+ — Quant fundamentals, Verbal vocabulary and reading, and timed practice. Free GRE mock tests included.

▶ Free College Predictor & study-abroad tools

What 320+ takes

A 320 usually means around 160 Quant and 160 Verbal. Quant is very learnable for most test-takers because the maths is high-school level — the challenge is speed, traps and careful reading. Verbal rewards vocabulary and reading stamina.

Quant: master fundamentals + traps

Revise arithmetic, algebra, ratios, percentages, exponents, geometry and basic statistics. Then drill the question types — Quantitative Comparison, multiple-answer and numeric entry. Most lost points come from misreading the question or falling for tempting wrong options, not hard maths.

Verbal: vocabulary + reading

Learn high-frequency GRE vocabulary in context (not flashcards alone) for Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence. For Reading Comprehension, practise identifying the main idea, author's tone and logical structure. Read dense non-fiction to build stamina.

Analytical Writing

The Issue essay asks for a reasoned argument on a claim. Focus on a clear position, well-developed reasons and specific examples. A clear structure scores well; you do not need elaborate prose.

Timed practice is non-negotiable

The GRE is adaptive by section and time-pressured. Sit full, timed mock tests to build pacing and learn when to guess and move on. Review every error to find patterns.
