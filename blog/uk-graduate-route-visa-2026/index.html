UK Graduate Route Visa 2026: Work Authorization for International Students

The UK Graduate Route allows international students to stay and work in the UK for up to 2 years after graduation (3 years for PhD graduates) without requiring a job offer or sponsorship. This is one of the most generous post-study work permits globally, allowing graduates to search for jobs, work for any employer, or start a business.

The Graduate Route is available to all eligible graduates, regardless of salary threshold or job field.

Who Qualifies for the UK Graduate Route in 2026?

To be eligible for the Graduate Route, you must meet ALL of the following criteria:

🔑
No job offer required: Unlike other countries, the UK Graduate Route requires NO job offer, salary threshold, or employer sponsorship. You can search for jobs while in the UK.
  • Student visa holder: Held a valid UK Student visa (or Isle of Man, Jersey, or Guernsey student visa) while studying
  • Completed degree: Successfully completed a degree program at an approved UK institution (bachelor's, master's, or doctorate)
  • RQF Level 6+: Degree must be at UK Qualification Framework Level 6 or above (typically bachelor's degree or higher)
  • Application timing: Apply for Graduate Route within 2 weeks of graduation OR while still in valid student visa status (before visa expiration)
  • No criminal conviction: Not subject to deportation; no serious criminal record
  • English language requirement: Already met while studying; no separate TOEFL/IELTS required

Graduate Route Duration: 2 Years (3 Years for PhD)

Graduate Route validity depends on your degree level:

  • 2-year Graduate Route: For all undergraduate and master's degrees (bachelor's, MSc, MA, MBA, etc.)
  • 3-year Graduate Route: For PhD and doctoral research degrees only
  • Full-time or part-time work: Work any hours—no restrictions like student visa (max 20 hours/week study requirement)
  • Self-employment: Start your own business, freelance work allowed
  • Job switching: Change employers unlimited times; no sponsorship or visa application required

Graduate Route Work Restrictions Lifted (2024 Update)

As of 2024, the UK removed salary thresholds and job list restrictions for Graduate Route holders—you can now work in ANY job role, not just shortage occupations.

  • No salary minimum: Previously required £20,800+ salary for some routes; no longer applies to Graduate Route
  • All occupations eligible: Can work as retail assistant, waiter, nurse, engineer, IT professional—any UK job
  • No job list requirement: Not limited to 'Shortage Occupation List' or specific sectors
  • Self-employment: Run your own business (sole proprietor, limited company, partnership)
  • Freelancing: Work as independent contractor for multiple clients simultaneously

Bringing Dependants on the Graduate Route (2024 Changes)

As of 2024, dependent spouse/partner and children rules for Graduate Route have been tightened. Eligibility is very limited:

⚠️
2024 dependent restrictions: The UK significantly tightened dependent eligibility on Graduate Route. If you're married or have children after arriving in the UK, they cannot join you on your Graduate Route visa. Dependants must have already been dependants before you applied for your student visa.

Graduate Route Visa Application Process

Apply for Graduate Route within 2 weeks of graduating or while in valid student status:

  1. Step 1: Obtain confirmation of graduation from your university (transcript or graduation letter with date and degree details)
  2. Step 2: Check your eligibility: degree level RQF 6+, valid student visa at time of application
  3. Step 3: Visit UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) portal on gov.uk and create account or log in
  4. Step 4: Select 'Apply for Graduate Route' visa in the portal; choose 'Inside the UK' application (if still in UK) or 'Outside the UK' (if departed)
  5. Step 5: Complete online application form: personal details, passport info, university details, graduation date, dependant information if any
  6. Step 6: Upload required documents: valid passport, graduation transcript/letter, current student visa/BRP (Biometric Residence Permit), proof of address in UK
  7. Step 7: Pay application fee: £719 (as of 2026; includes Immigration Health Surcharge [IHS] of approximately £284/year)
  8. Step 8: Submit application and receive confirmation with transaction ID
  9. Step 9: Wait for decision (typically 4–8 weeks if applying from UK; 8–12 weeks from outside UK)
  10. Step 10: Receive Graduate Route visa letter and new BRP in post (if in UK) or visa vignette in passport (if applied from abroad)

Work Rights & Restrictions on Graduate Route

Once you receive your Graduate Route visa, you have extensive work freedoms:

  • Unlimited hours: Work full-time, part-time, multiple jobs simultaneously
  • Any employer: No sponsorship required; work for any UK employer without visa application
  • Job switching: Change employers anytime with no notification to UKVI
  • Self-employment: Register as sole trader, start limited company, or freelance work
  • Sector flexibility: Work in any industry—no restrictions based on job list or shortage occupations
  • Remote work: Can work remotely for UK employers or international companies while in UK
  • Study: Can study full-time or part-time while working on Graduate Route (no work restrictions like student visa)

Transitioning from Graduate Route to Skilled Worker Visa

The Skilled Worker visa is the primary pathway from Graduate Route to permanent residency (PR) in the UK. Many graduates transition from Graduate Route (2–3 years work) to Skilled Worker sponsorship.

🔑
Graduate Route → Skilled Worker → PR timeline: Year 0–2: Graduate Route work. Year 2–5: Skilled Worker visa sponsorship. Year 5: Apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (PR). Year 6: Apply for British citizenship.
  • Skilled Worker visa requirements: Job offer from UK employer, salary of at least £26,200 (or higher for specific roles), employer sponsorship license
  • Timeline: Search for Skilled Worker sponsor employer during your 2-year Graduate Route period; once you have an offer and sponsorship, apply for Skilled Worker visa
  • Continuous residence: Time on Graduate Route counts toward Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR/PR) if you transition to Skilled Worker within allowed timeframe
  • ILR pathway: 5 years in UK on work visas → eligible for Indefinite Leave to Remain (PR); 6 years → eligible for British citizenship
  • Salary requirement: Entry-level salary is £26,200+; specialist roles have higher requirements. Check your job role on Skilled Worker list

Frequently asked questions

What is the UK Graduate Route visa duration in 2026?
**2 years** for bachelor's and master's degrees. **3 years** for PhD or other doctorate degrees. No extension available—you must transition to another visa category (Skilled Worker, Spouse) after expiration.
Do I need a job offer to apply for the Graduate Route?
**No**, the Graduate Route requires NO job offer, sponsorship, or salary threshold. You can apply immediately after graduation and search for jobs while in the UK.
Can I work any job on the Graduate Route visa?
Yes, **any UK job is permitted**—no salary minimum, no job list restrictions. You can work as retail assistant, nurse, engineer, IT professional, or start your own business. Completely flexible.
Can I bring my spouse/dependants on the Graduate Route?
**Very limited**. Only dependants who were already dependants BEFORE you arrived for your student visa can join. Spouses married after arriving cannot join. Confirm specific rules with UKVI.
How much does the Graduate Route cost?
**£719** total (approx. USD 900 or ₹75,000) including the application fee and Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS). IHS gives you access to UK NHS healthcare.
When should I apply for the Graduate Route?
Apply **within 2 weeks of graduation** or **while you still have valid student visa status**. Applying after your student visa expires may disqualify you or require separate immigration application.
What happens after my 2-year Graduate Route expires?
After expiration, you must transition to another visa: **Skilled Worker** (job sponsorship, PR pathway), **Spouse visa** (marriage), **Innovator/Start-Up visa** (business), or **Student visa** (further study). No extensions available.
How does Graduate Route lead to UK permanent residency?
Graduate Route alone does NOT lead to PR. You must transition to **Skilled Worker visa** with employer sponsorship (5 years total needed). After 5 years continuous UK residence on work visas, you're eligible for **Indefinite Leave to Remain (PR)**.
