Duolingo English Test (DET): Complete Guide to Format, Scoring & Prep

Master the Duolingo English Test with our comprehensive guide covering adaptive sections, scoring (10-160), all question types, cost, at-home delivery, and expert prep strategies for Indian students.

What is the Duolingo English Test (DET)?

The Duolingo English Test is a modern, AI-powered English proficiency exam administered entirely online. Developed by Duolingo, the language-learning company trusted by millions, the DET offers a faster, more affordable alternative to traditional tests like IELTS and TOEFL.

The test is proctored remotely via video and takes just 1 hour to complete. Unlike IELTS (3.5 hours) or TOEFL (3 hours), the DET is fast, affordable at ₹1,900 (~$23 USD), and accepted by major universities worldwide. For Indian students preparing for US, UK, Canada, and Australia study, the DET removes barriers of time and cost while delivering results within 48 hours.

DET Scoring Scale: 10-160

The Duolingo English Test uses a single unified score from 10 to 160, which makes it different from IELTS (band 1-9) or TOEFL (0-120).

Score Bands & What They Mean: - 10–24: Elementary (A0–A1) - 25–49: Lower Intermediate (A2) - 50–74: Intermediate (B1) - 75–99: Upper Intermediate (B2) - 100–124: Advanced (C1) - 125–160: Mastery (C2)

Most universities accept scores of 100–120 for undergraduate admission. Graduate programs (MS, MBA) typically require 115–130+. The score is reported instantly on your dashboard and emailed to institutions within 48 hours at no extra cost.

🔑
DET 100 ≈ IELTS 7.0 ≈ TOEFL iBT 93. Score validity is 2 years from the test date.

DET Test Format & Adaptive Sections

The Duolingo English Test is fully adaptive, meaning the difficulty of questions increases or decreases based on your performance in real-time. This makes the test fair and efficient—you're never stuck answering questions that are too easy or impossibly hard.

Test Duration: 1 hour - Section 1 (Interactive Review): ~10 minutes (warm-up, low stakes) - Section 2 (Adaptive Exam): ~50 minutes (main test, difficulty adapts)

Key Adaptive Features: - If you answer correctly, the next question is harder - If you answer incorrectly, the next question is easier - Your overall score reflects your true English level - No penalty for wrong answers

The adaptive algorithm assesses your skills across four dimensions: vocabulary, grammar, conversation fluency, and comprehension. This holistic approach ensures universities get a complete picture of your English ability.

ℹ️
Because the test is adaptive, the exact questions you see differ from other test-takers. You cannot predict or memorize the test.

All DET Question Types Explained

The DET mixes 8 different question types to test all four language skills. Here's what to expect:

1. Sentence Completion — Read an incomplete sentence and choose the best word or phrase (vocabulary, grammar)

2. Sentence Correction — Identify and fix errors in a sentence (grammar, syntax)

3. Passage Comprehension — Read a short text and answer multiple-choice questions (reading, inference)

4. Listening Comprehension — Hear audio and answer questions about the content (listening, note-taking)

5. Conversation Preview — Choose the best response to a conversational prompt (speaking, fluency)

6. Interactive Speaking — Respond naturally to follow-up questions in a mini-conversation (fluency, accent, response relevance)

7. English Proficiency Speaking — Deliver a 1-2 minute spoken response to a prompt (organization, vocabulary, grammar, fluency)

8. Cloze / Fill-in-the-Blank — Complete a passage with missing words based on context (vocabulary, comprehension)

No single question type dominates; the test rotates through all eight to ensure balanced assessment. Your score is a composite that reflects how well you performed across all skills.

  • No essay writing — all questions are objective or short-form audio responses
  • No handwriting — everything is typed or spoken into a microphone
  • Real-world contexts — conversations, news clips, academic texts are authentic
  • AI-graded speaking — automated speech recognition evaluates pronunciation, fluency, and accuracy

How Much Does the DET Cost?

The Duolingo English Test is the cheapest official English proficiency exam available to Indian students.

DET Pricing (India): - Test Fee: ₹1,900 (~$23 USD) per attempt - Score Report to Universities: FREE (included, no per-school fee) - Retake Fee: ₹1,900 per attempt (same price, no discounts for retakes) - Score Report Extensions: FREE (send old scores to new universities anytime)

Comparison to Other Tests: - IELTS: ₹13,000–14,000 per attempt + ₹150–200 per institution report - TOEFL iBT: ₹17,000–18,000 per attempt + ₹17 per report (max 4 free) - PTE Academic: ₹11,500–12,500 per attempt + ₹40–100 per report - DET: ₹1,900 + FREE reporting (saves ₹10,000+ per attempt)

For Indian students, the DET eliminates cost as a barrier to English proficiency certification. You can afford to take it 2–3 times to reach your target score without breaking your study budget.

💡
Payment via Indian debit/credit cards is instant. You can schedule a test date within 24 hours of paying, with flexible dates available every day.

Taking the DET: At-Home Proctored Test

One of the biggest advantages of the DET is that you take it from home—no test center, no travel, no 3.5-hour exam marathon in a crowded room.

At-Home Setup Requirements: - Device: Laptop or desktop with webcam, microphone, and speakers (no phones) - Internet: Stable broadband (5+ Mbps download, 2+ Mbps upload) - Browser: Chrome or Edge (not Safari or Firefox) - Environment: Quiet room, well-lit, empty desk except for water and ID - ID Verification: Valid passport or government-issued ID (screenshot for online proctoring)

What Happens During the Test: 1. You download the DET app and create an account with your email 2. Proctors monitor via webcam—they watch for cheating (no phone, no notes, no talking) 3. You speak answers aloud into your microphone (AI evaluates pronunciation and fluency) 4. Proctors can ask you to show your desk/surroundings if they're suspicious 5. Test ends, and results appear in your account within 1–2 days (usually 24 hours)

No Travel Needed: - Save ₹500–1,000 on test-center travel (IELTS, TOEFL require commutes) - No exam day stress from commute; test in your comfortable home - Schedule tests any day of the year (weekends included) at any time - Retake within days if needed, without traveling again

⚠️
Proctors take violations seriously. Do not use translators, have others in the room, or reference notes. Caught cheating = test void, fee lost, account banned. Keep it honest.

Step-by-Step: How to Register & Schedule Your DET

Ready to take the DET? Here's the exact process:

  1. Go to www.duolingo.com/english-test (or use the Duolingo mobile app).
  2. Click 'Get Started' and create a Duolingo account (use a valid email you check regularly).
  3. Verify your email and fill in your profile (name, date of birth, country = India).
  4. Pay the ₹1,900 fee via debit card, credit card, or UPI (all accepted for Indian users).
  5. You'll instantly see available test dates/times for the next 30 days. Pick one. Times range from 6 AM to 10 PM IST.
  6. Confirm your choice. You'll receive a confirmation email with test instructions and tech-check link.
  7. 2–3 days before: Open the tech-check link to verify your camera, mic, and internet work properly.
  8. On test day: Log in 15 minutes early. Proctor will verify your ID, ID number, and test environment.
  9. Complete the 1-hour exam. Results email within 24–48 hours.
  10. Review your score on your Duolingo dashboard. Send scores to universities instantly (no fees, no limits).

DET Preparation Strategy: Tips & Resources

The DET is adaptive and AI-graded, so preparation is different from IELTS or TOEFL. Here's how to prep effectively:

1. Use Duolingo Itself (Free Resource) Duolingo's platform is excellent for building vocabulary, grammar, and conversational fluency. Spend 30 minutes daily on Duolingo lessons (Stories, Podcasts, Lessons) for 4–6 weeks. The app teaches real English and gets your ear used to native accents.

2. Practice Listening & Speaking (Critical) The DET heavily weights fluency and accent clarity. Practice by: - Watching English movies/shows without subtitles - Recording yourself speaking—read news articles aloud for 2 minutes daily - Using platforms like YouTube (TED talks, podcasts) to train your ear - Speaking with native speakers (Tandem, HelloTalk apps)

3. Vocabulary Expansion Focus on academic vocabulary (especially if aiming for 110+): - Anki flashcard app: pre-made DET vocabulary decks - Reading: The Guardian, BBC News, Medium articles on topics you like - Learn 2–3 words daily; use them in sentences you speak aloud

4. Grammar Drills Grammar errors hurt your score in multiple sections. Focus on: - Subject-verb agreement - Tense consistency (past/present/future) - Article use (a/an/the) - Prepositions Use websites like Khan Academy or BBC Learning English (free) for targeted practice.

5. Mock Tests / Official Practice Duolingo offers free official practice exams in your account. Take one full mock test 1 week before your real test to: - Get used to the adaptive flow - Time-manage (you have exactly 50 minutes for the main section) - Identify weak areas (grammar? speaking clarity? vocabulary?)

6. Speaking Clarity AI rates your accent and clarity highly. Practice by: - Slowing down your speech (rushing loses points) - Enunciating consonants clearly - Recording yourself and listening back for mumbling or unclear words - Practicing the specific accents/phonemes that trip you up

🔑
For Indian test-takers: The AI recognizes Indian English accents. You don't need a British or American accent—just clear, intelligible speech. Clarity > Accent.
  • Prep timeline: 4–8 weeks for scores 90–100; 8–12 weeks for 110+
  • Study 1–2 hours daily (30 min Duolingo, 30 min listening, 30 min speaking/grammar)
  • Take at least 2 official practice tests before the real one
  • Rest 2–3 days before exam day; don't cram the night before
  • Test early in the day when your mind is freshest (6–9 AM time slots)

DET vs IELTS vs TOEFL: Which Should You Take?

If you're unsure whether to take DET, IELTS, or TOEFL, here's the breakdown:

Duolingo English Test (DET): - Cost: ₹1,900 (cheapest) - Duration: 1 hour (fastest) - Format: Fully online, at-home (most convenient) - Results: 24–48 hours (instant) - Acceptance: 4,000+ universities (growing) - Best For: Quick, budget-friendly option; flexibility-focused students

IELTS: - Cost: ₹13,000–14,000 per attempt - Duration: 3.5 hours (longest) - Format: Test center or online (online less common) - Results: 10–13 days - Acceptance: 11,000+ universities, UK/Australia/Canada gold standard - Best For: UK, Australia, Canada study; well-established reputation

TOEFL iBT: - Cost: ₹17,000–18,000 per attempt - Duration: 3 hours - Format: Test center or online (online available) - Results: 6 days - Acceptance: 10,000+ universities, especially US - Best For: US universities; academic credibility

Choose DET if: You want fast results, affordable cost, and maximum flexibility (at-home test, anytime). Increasingly accepted at top US, UK, Canada universities.

Choose IELTS if: You're applying to Australia, UK, or Canada; want the safest, most widely accepted option; don't mind paying more.

Choose TOEFL if: You're targeting the USA; have access to a test center; prefer a longer, more familiar format.

Common DET FAQs

Q: Can I retake the DET immediately? A: Yes. You can schedule another test as soon as 24 hours after your previous attempt. Unlike IELTS (wait months for next date), DET lets you retake whenever you're ready.

Q: What if my internet cuts out during the test? A: You have grace period of 2–5 minutes. If you reconnect within this window, you continue. If not, the test is voided and you lose your fee (no refund). Always test your internet quality 48 hours before.

Q: Can I use a phone to take the DET? A: No. DET only works on laptops/desktops with a webcam. Phones are not supported and proctors will flag them as cheating attempts.

Q: Is a score of 100 good enough for US universities? A: For most universities, yes (100 ≈ IELTS 7.0 ≈ TOEFL 93). Top-tier schools (Stanford, MIT, Harvard) may prefer 110–120+. Check your university's minimum before applying.

Q: How is DET different from Duolingo English lessons (the app)? A: Duolingo lessons are practice tools (free/paid subscription). The Duolingo English Test (DET) is the official proficiency exam (₹1,900 per attempt). They're related but separate products.

Q: Do universities trust the DET as much as IELTS/TOEFL? A: Yes and increasingly more. Major universities (Yale, UT Austin, University of Toronto, ANU) explicitly list DET as acceptable. However, IELTS/TOEFL have longer track records. Ask your target university if they accept DET before applying.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Duolingo English Test recognized by universities in the USA?
Yes. Major US universities including Yale, Stanford, UT Austin, and University of Arizona explicitly accept the DET. Most US universities now accept scores 100+. Check your specific university's website for their minimum requirement.
How long is the Duolingo English Test?
The DET takes exactly **1 hour** to complete (50 minutes of adaptive exam + 10 minutes of interactive review). This is significantly shorter than IELTS (3.5 hours) or TOEFL iBT (3 hours).
What is a good DET score for masters programs?
Most US/UK/Canada master's programs require **scores between 110–125**. Top-tier universities (MIT, Harvard, Oxford) may prefer 120+. Check your program's specific requirement; the minimum varies by university and field.
Can I take the DET from home in India?
Yes, absolutely. The DET is a fully online, at-home proctored exam. You need a laptop, webcam, microphone, and stable internet. No test centers or travel required.
How quickly can I get my DET score?
Results are typically available **within 24–48 hours** of completing the test. This is much faster than IELTS (10–13 days) or TOEFL (6 days).
How much does the Duolingo English Test cost in India?
The DET costs **₹1,900 per attempt** in India. Score reports to universities are completely free (no per-institution fee). This makes it the cheapest English proficiency test available.
What if I fail the DET? Can I retake it?
Yes. You can retake the DET as many times as you need. You can schedule another test **within 24 hours** of your previous attempt and pay the ₹1,900 fee again. There's no waiting period like IELTS.
Is DET harder than IELTS?
No, not harder—just different. DET is **adaptive** (gets harder/easier based on your answers), fully online, and heavily tests **speaking clarity**. IELTS is standardized, paper/pencil-based, and tests written English more. Neither is definitively 'harder.'
How do I prepare for the DET?
Use Duolingo app daily (30 min), practice speaking aloud (30 min), watch English movies/podcasts (30 min), and take official free mock tests every 2 weeks. Prep timeline is typically 4–8 weeks for 90–100 scores, 8–12 weeks for 110+.
