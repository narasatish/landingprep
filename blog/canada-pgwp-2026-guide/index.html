Canada Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) 2026: Complete Guide

The Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) is one of the most valuable benefits of studying in Canada. It allows graduates to work legally in Canada for up to 3 years after graduation, and this work experience directly counts toward Canadian permanent residency (PR) applications.

PGWP is a critical pathway for Indian students to transition from study to work to PR in Canada.

Who is Eligible for PGWP in 2026?

To be eligible for a PGWP, you must meet ALL of the following criteria:

🔑
180-day application window: You MUST apply for PGWP within 180 days of receiving your graduation document. Missing this deadline means losing PGWP eligibility permanently.
  • Study in Canada: Completed a full-time program (minimum 8 months) at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) in Canada
  • Valid study permit: Held a valid study permit while studying and while applying for PGWP
  • Completed studies: Successfully graduated with a diploma or degree (confirmation from institution)
  • Program length: Studied for a minimum of 8 months in a program designated as PGWP-eligible
  • Time limit: Apply within 180 days of graduation or after receiving proof of graduation—whichever comes first

Field-of-Study Requirement: Which Programs are PGWP-Eligible?

As of 2024, a major change: PGWP is limited to graduates from programs in priority fields. The government restricts PGWP to healthcare, skilled trades, and technology.

Note: Rules are changing for 2026–2027. Verify current eligible fields on canada.ca before enrolling.

⚠️
2024–2026 policy changes: Canada has restricted PGWP to priority fields to address labor shortages. Rules have changed and may continue to evolve. Always confirm with your institution and Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) before committing to a program.
  • GENERALLY eligible programs: Healthcare (nursing, medicine, therapy), STEM (engineering, computer science, mathematics), skilled trades (plumbing, welding, construction)
  • Often restricted (confirm): Business administration, hospitality, social sciences without STEM focus, pure liberal arts
  • Eligible institution status: Your university must be on Canada's Designated Learning Institution (DLI) list—most Canadian universities are DLI; international branch campuses may not be
  • PGWP eligibility confirmation: Contact your institution's international office BEFORE enrolling to confirm your program qualifies for PGWP

Language Requirements for PGWP Application

PGWP applications do NOT require a separate English/French language test score. However, if you took your studies in a language other than English or French, IRCC may request evidence that you completed the program in that language.

  • If you studied in English or French: No language requirement for PGWP—submit graduation documents
  • If you studied in another language: Provide official translation of diploma/degree (notarized English translation), plus evidence from institution that instruction was in that language
  • Bilingual programs: No additional language test required

Step-by-Step PGWP Application Process

Follow this process to apply for your PGWP within 180 days of graduation:

  1. Step 1: Obtain official graduation confirmation from your institution (typically provided with your diploma or available from the registrar)
  2. Step 2: Gather required documents: valid passport, study permit, proof of graduation, proof of identity, proof of residence in Canada (if applicable)
  3. Step 3: Create/log into your Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) account at canada.ca
  4. Step 4: Select 'Apply for Post-Graduation Work Permit' from the application options
  5. Step 5: Complete the online application form with personal details, study program info, institution name, graduation date
  6. Step 6: Upload all required documents (passport, study permit, graduation letter, notarized translations if needed)
  7. Step 7: Pay the application fee: CAD 255 (non-refundable)
  8. Step 8: Submit application online and receive confirmation email with your application reference number
  9. Step 9: Wait for processing (typically 4–6 weeks for online applications, 8–12 weeks for paper applications) and receive decision via IRCC portal or email

PGWP Duration & Work Restrictions

PGWP is typically valid for the same duration as your study program (up to 3 years maximum). However, it is NOT employer-specific—you can work for any employer in Canada.

  • PGWP duration: Equal to program length (minimum 1 year, maximum 3 years)
  • Employer flexibility: Work for any Canadian employer—can change jobs without notifying IRCC
  • Geographic flexibility: Work anywhere in Canada (no provincial restrictions)
  • Self-employment: PGWP permits self-employment and starting your own business
  • Part-time and full-time: Can work full-time, part-time, or multiple jobs simultaneously
  • Extensions: PGWP cannot be extended once expired—if you need to stay, apply for PR or another visa category before expiration

Transitioning from PGWP to Canadian Permanent Residency (PR)

The key advantage of PGWP is that the work experience earned counts directly toward PR applications through Express Entry.

Common pathway: 1. Graduate and obtain PGWP (work permit valid 1–3 years) 2. Work in a skilled occupation for 12–24 months (work experience qualifies you for Canadian Experience Class) 3. Apply for PR through Express Entry (your PGWP work experience + Canadian qualifications → higher points → faster approval) 4. Receive PR decision (typically 6 months) 5. Transition to Permanent Residency (PR), then citizenship after 3 years

🔑
PGWP = Express Entry fast-track: Work experience on PGWP directly counts toward Canadian Experience Class (CEC), which typically processes PR applications in 6 months. This is faster than many other PR pathways.

Common PGWP Application Issues & Solutions

Avoid these common mistakes when applying:

  • Missing the 180-day deadline: Mark your graduation date + 180 days on a calendar; apply 30–60 days early to avoid missing the deadline
  • Incorrect institution name: Double-check your institution's exact legal name on the DLI list; using a nickname or common name may cause rejection
  • Non-DLI institution: Confirm your university is on the DLI list before enrolling. Graduates of non-DLI institutions are NOT eligible for PGWP
  • Incomplete application: Submit ALL required documents in one package; incomplete applications are returned and delay processing by weeks
  • Language issues: If your degree was in a non-English/French language, obtain a notarized translation of your diploma AND written confirmation from your institution

Frequently asked questions

What is the PGWP duration for 2026?
**PGWP duration equals your program length**: A 2-year master's degree qualifies for a **3-year PGWP** (maximum). Shorter programs (8 months–1 year) qualify for PGWP up to that program duration. Maximum PGWP is **3 years**.
Am I eligible for PGWP if I studied online or outside Canada?
**No**, PGWP requires you to study in Canada at a **Designated Learning Institution (DLI)**. Online programs delivered by Canadian institutions may qualify, but international branch campuses often do NOT. Confirm eligibility with your institution.
Can I apply for PGWP if my study permit expired?
You must have held a valid study permit **while studying**. If your permit expires after graduation but before your PGWP application, you can still apply within 180 days. Restore status online if permit expired (apply from within Canada).
What is the 180-day deadline for PGWP applications?
You MUST apply for PGWP within **180 days of receiving your graduation document** (diploma, degree, or official graduation letter). Missing this deadline permanently disqualifies you—extensions are NOT granted.
Can I work anywhere in Canada on PGWP?
Yes, PGWP allows you to work **anywhere in Canada, for any employer, full-time or part-time**. You can change employers without notifying IRCC or reporting. No provincial restrictions.
Does PGWP require a specific job offer?
No, PGWP does NOT require a job offer. You can apply immediately after graduation and search for a job during the PGWP validity period. However, some employers prefer applicants with PGWP already in hand.
How does PGWP lead to permanent residency in Canada?
Work experience on PGWP counts toward the **Canadian Experience Class (CEC)** in Express Entry. After 12–24 months of skilled work on PGWP, you can apply for PR, which processes in ~6 months.
Is PGWP available for all Canadian degree programs?
No, PGWP is restricted to **priority fields** as of 2024: healthcare, STEM, and skilled trades. Business administration, hospitality, and non-STEM programs may not qualify. Confirm with your institution before enrolling.
