The quick recap

Canada s first Express Entry draw of July 2026 was held on 6 July 2026 — a Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) round that issued 534 invitations with a CRS cut-off of 708, reported as the lowest PNP cut-off so far this year. It followed a busy end to June, including a Canadian Experience Class (CEC) round at CRS 516 and category-based draws for healthcare and physicians. IRCC continues to favour category-based selection in 2026.

What the numbers mean for you

The spread between categories is the story of 2026:

  • PNP draws show very high cut-offs (708–730) because a provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points — the score reflects nominees, not the general pool.
  • CEC draws are steady around CRS 516 — the core route if you have Canadian work experience.
  • Category-based draws can invite much lower scores — the physician round went as low as CRS 223, and French-language rounds in 2026 have often cut off near 400.
  • Takeaway: if your CRS is below the CEC line, your fastest levers are a provincial nomination, stronger language (aim CLB 9), or qualifying for an in-demand category.
